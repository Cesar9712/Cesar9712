## Description
Bounty #16601 documents a two-phase settlement path for accepted distribution packages: the reward is queued first, then confirmation follows roughly 24 hours later, with `pending_id` and transaction evidence posted by the maintainers.

This Short separates the states that bounty hunters often mix together: submitted, accepted, and confirmed paid.

Source: https://github.com/Scottcjn/rustchain-bounties/issues/16601
